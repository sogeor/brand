Sogeor Red is used only for the mark and small accents, never for large fills or body text. The mark is red, white or black on a plain background; no gradients, outlines, shadows or other colors.

Contrast (WCAG): Sogeor Red on black — 4.6:1, on white — 4.5:1; suitable for the mark and large text, not for small body text on white.
